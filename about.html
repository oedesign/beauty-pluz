ABOUT BEAUTY PLUZ

Beauty, Thoughtfully Chosen.

Beauty Pluz is a UK-based beauty destination with African roots and a global outlook

We bring together a carefully selected range of skincare, haircare and beauty products from trusted brands, giving you more ways to discover products that suit your skin, your routine and your preferences.

Because we don't believe beauty should be one-size-fits-all.

And we don't believe more products automatically mean better choices.

"We believe in choosing well"
What Drives Us

Mission & Vision

We Curate

We know that no single brand can meet every beauty need. That's why we search for products from a range of beauty brands that offer something worthwhile—whether that's a thoughtful formulation, a useful ingredient, a different approach, or a solution for a specific concern. We've selected our collection to give you more choice while keeping the experience considered.

We Help You Discover

Beauty shouldn't feel like a research project.

We make it easier to explore products by presenting useful information clearly, helping you understand what you're considering before you add it to your routine.

Whether you're looking for everyday essentials or something new to try, we want your experience to feel simple, informed and personal.

OUR PROMISE

Beauty Should Feel Personal

From the products we select to the experience we create, we want every part of Beauty Pluz to feel considered.

We won't always have the answer for everyone.

But we'll keep looking for products worth discovering, information worth knowing, and choices worth making

Because you don't need more beauty.

Meet the Founder

The Person Behind the Formulas

Omowunmi Adejobi

Founder. Creative Entrepreneur. Beauty & Fashion Enthusiast

Omowunmi Adejobi brings a creative eye shaped by fashion, design and entrepreneurship to Beauty Pluz. Her passion for beauty goes beyond products. It is about *style, self-expression and the art of choosing well*. With Beauty Pluz, she brings her fashion-led perspective into beauty—curating skincare, haircare and beauty products that fit different tastes, routines and lifestyles. *Because, like fashion, beauty is personal. And the best choices are the ones that feel like you.
